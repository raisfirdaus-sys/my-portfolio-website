Investment Philosophy

Four pillars behind these 11 stocks

Mission

To prove that an individual investor can build a solid stock portfolio through independent research, discipline, and patience — then share that process openly on YouTube, Instagram, X, and LinkedIn so more people feel confident starting to invest the right way.

Values

  • Cross-sector diversification: technology, energy, healthcare, finance, and consumer staples.
  • Fundamental research before chasing trends or market hype.
  • Full transparency — including when a position is in the red.
  • A long-term horizon, not day trading.

Vision

Over the next 5 years, I want this 11-stock portfolio to grow through a combination of capital gains and reinvested dividends, backed by two megatrends I believe in: accelerating AI adoption & electrification (MU, GEV), plus an ever-expanding digital economy & global healthcare (MA, V, ABBV, JNJ, GILD) — anchored by stable consumer staples (PG, KO, MNST, VLO).

Action

My execution strategy: periodic dollar-cost averaging, reinvesting dividends, re-evaluating the thesis every quarter when earnings drop, and rebalancing if any one sector starts to dominate the portfolio unhealthily. I document every update on social media so it can double as a case study for anyone learning alongside me.

Investor Letter

A short note for anyone following this journey

This isn't about chasing a stock that 10x's overnight — it's 11 businesses I understand, a long-term growth path that makes sense, and letting compounding do the work.

I'm open about the allocation, the reasoning, and even the positions that are in the red — because the learning process is most valuable exactly when things aren't going perfectly. Thanks for following along on YouTube, Instagram, X, or LinkedIn. Let's learn and grow together.
