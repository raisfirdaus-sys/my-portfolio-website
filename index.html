    Investment Philosophy

    Four pillars behind these 12 stocks

    Mission

    To prove that an individual investor can build a solid stock portfolio through independent research, discipline, and patience — then share that process openly on YouTube, Instagram, X, and LinkedIn so more people feel confident starting to invest the right way.

    Values

    • Cross-sector diversification: technology, energy, healthcare, finance, and consumer staples.
    • Fundamental research before chasing trends or market hype.
    • Full transparency — including when a position is in the red.
    • A long-term horizon, not day trading.

    Vision

    Over the next 5 years, I want this 12-stock portfolio to grow through a combination of capital gains and reinvested dividends, backed by three megatrends I believe in: cloud/software & AI platforms (DDOG, MSFT, GOOGL, PLTR), digital payments & e-commerce (MA, V, AMZN), plus breakthrough healthcare & pharma innovation (ABBV, JNJ, GILD) — anchored by stable consumer staples and discretionary brands (KO, SBUX).

    Action

    My execution strategy: periodic dollar-cost averaging, reinvesting dividends, re-evaluating the thesis every quarter when earnings drop, and rebalancing if any one sector starts to dominate the portfolio unhealthily. I document every update on social media so it can double as a case study for anyone learning alongside me.
